The Problem

CryoET is transforming biology — but the data is overwhelming.

Cryo-electron tomography captures 3D snapshots of cells at near-atomic resolution. Each scan contains billions of voxels — a dense, complex volume of proteins, membranes, and molecular machines interacting in their native environment.

The bottleneck: scientists spend weeks manually identifying and labeling individual molecules — ribosomes, viruses, enzymes — buried inside these massive 3D volumes. There hasn't been a simple, accessible way to see, search, and make sense of it all.

What QBI Does

From raw data to biological insight — in one place.

QBI is a web-based platform that combines interactive 3D visualization with AI-powered molecule detection, giving scientists an intuitive way to explore cryoET data and get answers fast.

📦 Upload Zarr Scan → 🧬 AI Detects Molecules → 🔬 Explore in 3D → 📊 Get Structured Insights
🖱️

Interactive 3D Viewer

Orbit, zoom, and slice through tomograms in real time. Navigate X, Y, and Z axes independently to inspect every layer of the volume.

🤖

AI-Powered Detection

One click runs a deep learning model that identifies and classifies molecules — ribosomes, viruses, proteins — across the entire scan.

🏷️

Label Management

Upload expert-curated labels, browse detected molecules by type, inspect individual picks, and validate AI predictions visually.

📋

Automated Analysis Reports

Get structured insights: molecule counts, clustering patterns, spatial distribution, and caveats — generated automatically from your data.

Key Features

What makes QBI different.

🌀

Real-time 3D Volume Rendering

Navigate massive Zarr datasets in the browser with smooth, interactive controls. No downloads, no specialized hardware — just a web browser.

✂️

Orthogonal Slice Views

Scroll through X, Y, and Z cross-sections simultaneously. Detected molecules are overlaid directly on the slices with color-coded highlights.

🧠

One-Click AI Inference

Run a trained 3D segmentation model directly from the interface. Real-time progress tracking with live tiling feedback — no terminal needed.

📊

Intelligent Analysis Engine

Automatically generates structured reports: molecule frequency, spatial clustering, singleton analysis, and validation recommendations.

📂

Upload Any Zarr

Drag-and-drop your own .zarr folders or point to a local path. Supports multi-resolution (mipmap) levels for fast preview at any scale.

🔗

Export & Share

Export reports, open scans in 3D Slicer, or share findings with collaborators. The web server handles all the heavy lifting.
